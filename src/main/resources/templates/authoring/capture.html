    JAB element

    Click a control on the screenshot to read it from SystmOne's accessibility tree. Attribute locators survive a screen redraw, a theme change and a different resolution, so prefer one over a template wherever the control exposes itself.

    Reads whatever SystmOne itself considers focused, so no clicking on the screenshot is needed: tab to a field in SystmOne, come back here and press the button. The browser holding the desktop's focus does not change what SystmOne's own tree calls focused.
